| Infinite delegation loops | JARVIS↔HELIX↔AJ↔Hermes routing without hop limits/ownership rules | No integrated guard observed | Medium-High | Max depth/hop count, delegation graph checks, ownership constraints |

## 4. Honest lifecycle assessment
- **AJ internal lifecycle:** mature and evidence-oriented for controlled local use, but still non-atomic across stores and not exactly-once for external effects.
- **Cross-ecosystem lifecycle:** **conceptual only**. Critical transitions (submission, dispatch, progress, completion delivery, cancel propagation, identity/authz, correlation) lack implemented and verified contracts.
- **Safety requirement for integration:** all cross-component states must be treated as `unknown` until backed by correlated evidence; no optimistic completion. Slow, correct, auditable integration is required over fast wiring.
