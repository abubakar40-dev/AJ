## 3. Specific questions (requested)
- **Can JARVIS request privileged AJ actions without a trusted authorization path?** **Yes, in effect today there is no integrated trusted path**; AJ reconcile is locally guarded but not authenticated, and no cross-component authorization contract exists. Any future remote/in-process bridge must fail closed.
- **Can HELIX bypass JARVIS's intended routing/user permission policy?** **Potentially/ambiguously yes** due to independent runtimes/copies and lack of enforced ecosystem routing contract (evidence insufficient to prove enforcement).
- **Can Hermes skills execute outside approved permissions?** **Cannot be ruled out for AJ-integrated use** (and Hermes is independently operable); Hermes has controls, but AJ-boundary enforcement/coverage unverified.
- **Can a task result be forged or misrepresented?** **Risk exists** without signed/evidence-mapped cross-component results and correlation; AJ mitigates internally with evidence, but external mappings are undefined.
- **Can one component impersonate another?** **Possible where identity is metadata/trust-by-configuration only**; no verified mutual identity.
- **Are credentials unnecessarily shared?** **Not fully determinable read-only without secret inspection/disclosure**; multiple config/env stores suggest sprawl risk—treat as medium operational concern.
- **Could a compromised component affect the entire ecosystem?** **Yes, materially**, given shared user context, local filesystem/config access, and independently powerful runtimes (Hermes tools/skills/platforms, JARVIS automation).

## 4. Overall security posture
**Controlled-local, component-isolated in intent but not securely integrated.** AJ core made meaningful 3F progress for its own privileged boundary; ecosystem-level integration remains **high risk** without explicit contracts, identity propagation, least-privilege mediation, and evidence-verified execution boundaries. No unsupported claim of secure end-to-end integration is warranted.
