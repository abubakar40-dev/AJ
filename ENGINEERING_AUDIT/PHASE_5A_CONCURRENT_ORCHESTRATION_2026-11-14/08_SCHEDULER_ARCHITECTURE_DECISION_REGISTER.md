|---|---|---|---|

## B. Proposed architecture decisions (Stage 5A.2)

| ID | Proposed decision | Existing source evidence | Alternatives considered | Rationale | Trade-offs | Reliability implications | Resource implications | Security implications | Needed in first implementation or deferrable | Confidence | Evidence that could change recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SD-01 | Introduce an explicit **admission-control layer** distinct from task creation, authorization, eligibility, dispatch, and execution; represent waiting-for-capacity as deferred/backpressured (not failed) via durable admission decision/evidence | No admission concept in `scheduler.py`/`orchestrator.py`; `AUTHORIZED` conflated with runnable; 06 scenarios 2,3,6,12 | (a) Treat `AUTHORIZED` as admitted; (b) add `ADMITTED` task state immediately; (c) in-memory-only gating | Makes capacity/safety gating explicit and auditable; prevents unsafe dispatch and semantic confusion | More components/records; policy complexity | Improves correctness/fail-closed behavior if durable; risk if evidence not persisted | Enables bounded load on 8 GB host | Preserves authz boundary; supports deny/defer reasons | **Needed early** (design/contract in first impl.) | High | Evidence that current task states can represent admission/revocation without ambiguity |
| SD-02 | Adopt a conceptual pipeline `Authorized → Dependency/Readiness (Eligible) → Admission Check → Admitted/Deferred → Dispatch intent → Lease/Assignment → Execution → Outcome/Recovery`, with eligibility separate from admission | 06 §§1.2–1.4, task/resume/dependency behavior | (a) Single-step schedule; (b) merge eligibility into dispatch only | Clarifies gates, supports reevaluation and observability, aligns with 5A.1 domain separation | More transitions/events to manage | Better reasoning under restart/partial failure | Avoids wasted dispatch attempts | Clear policy checkpoints | **Needed early** (logical model) | High | Simpler model proven sufficient with equal safety/evidence |
| SD-03 | Use **hierarchical static budgets** for first version: global active/admitted caps + per-task/per-capability/(future per-agent/runtime)/resource-class caps + bounded retries/backoff; no arbitrary numeric defaults in this doc | `workers/limit` only in scheduler; no resource model; 8 GB/2C4T | (a) Global cap only; (b) fully dynamic telemetry-driven control; (c) unbounded best-effort | Simplest safe model that can evolve; prevents fan-out/retry storms on small host | Static caps may under/over-utilize; tuning needed | Predictable containment of concurrency | Direct control of memory/CPU/IO pressure | Limits blast radius per capability/runtime | **Needed in first implementation** | Medium-High | Measured workload evidence supporting different hierarchy/defaults |
| SD-04 | Make admission **resource-aware and persistence-health-gated** (fail closed on unknown/unhealthy storage or unavailable measurements for non-trivial work); require durable intent before dispatch | NTFS/WAL risk, non-atomic stores, 06 scenario 12, storage diagnostics | (a) Best-effort dispatch; (b) monitor-only (no gating); (c) assume WAL reliable | Prevents compounding failures on unreliable storage/pressure; honest behavior | Reduced availability/throughput under uncertainty | Major reduction in ambiguous/undetected loss risk | Protects RAM/disk/IO on constrained host | Avoids unsafe actions without evidence | **Needed in first implementation** | High | Demonstrated reliable storage/metrics eliminating need for fail-closed gating |
| SD-05 | First-version scheduling policy: **priority bands + FIFO within band + per-task/per-capability fairness caps + simple aging/anti-starvation**, deadlines as hint/gate (not full EDF initially) | Current sort by priority then task_id only; no fairness/aging | (a) Pure FIFO; (b) strict priority; (c) EDF; (d) complex weighted fair scheduler v1 | Practical, deterministic, low complexity, addresses starvation risk acceptably for v1 | Not optimal for all deadline mixes; requires tuning | Predictable behavior; easier recovery reasoning | Modest overhead | Deterministic/auditable decisions | **Needed in first implementation** (basic form) | Medium | Workload evidence requiring EDF/complex WFQ immediately |
| SD-06 | Dependencies are **correctness gates**: typed/explicit satisfaction semantics (incl. cycles, failed/cancelled/unknown/recovery-required handling), no confusion of task vs execution deps, parent completion is aggregate not dispatch-implied | `depends_on` task-level checks/cycles exist but passive; 5A.1 PD-06; 06 scenarios 7,8 | (a) Scheduler-order only; (b) ignore unknown dep states; (c) full DAG engine in v1 | Prevents unsafe unblocking and false completion | Graph evaluation complexity; eventing needed later | Avoids cascading incorrect execution | Extra evaluation cost | Prevents unauthorized/unsafe progression | **Partially needed early** (enforce semantics), full graph engine **deferrable** | Medium-High | Proof that all workflows fit simple task-only deps with passive checks |
| SD-07 | Recommend **Option D: Hybrid staged approach** (start with Option A single-process foundations + explicit interfaces; evolve to worker-manager/process isolation only if justified) | Current in-process helper, 8 GB host, NTFS lock/WAL risks, modular monolith constraints | Option A only, B now, C now, broker/distributed systems | Best fit for constraints/migration/risk; avoids premature infra | Slower path to strong isolation; requires discipline on interfaces | Incremental hardening possible; SPOF initially | Lowest initial overhead | Smaller attack/ops surface initially | **Architectural direction for first implementation** | High | Evidence of sustained need for multi-process isolation outweighing costs/risks |
| SD-08 | Define scheduler observability as **durable admission/dispatch/recovery events + derived views**, clearly separating durable facts from transient metrics; do not invent uncollectible metrics | Existing stores/diagnostics partial; no scheduler metrics; 4B evidence culture | (a) Logs only; (b) in-memory dashboard metrics only; (c) external telemetry stack | Auditability and restart diagnosis consistent with AJ model | Additional event volume/storage writes | Improves diagnosis/reconciliation | Write/storage pressure (must be bounded) | Must preserve redaction/no-secret leakage in events | **Partially needed early** (key events), rich metrics **deferrable** | High | Operational evidence requiring external telemetry platform (not default) |
| SD-09 | Shutdown/restart/recovery policy: stop admission on shutdown/pressure, persist intent before dispatch, classify ambiguous/expired-lease outcomes conservatively, allow replay only when evidence proves safety; **never exactly-once external claims** | `recovery.py`/leases/receipts behavior, 06 scenarios 5,6,9–12 | (a) Auto-retry on restart; (b) drop in-flight tasks; (c) assume completion | Matches fail-closed/evidence-honest invariants and unknown external effects | More manual-review cases; lower apparent automation | Correctness over convenience | Recovery throttling under pressure | Prevents unsafe duplicate side effects | **Needed in first implementation** | High | Strong external idempotency/cancellation contracts with verified evidence (future, scoped) |
| SD-10 | Keep scheduler/admission compatible with future concurrent orchestration and Hermes execution **without implementing Hermes lifecycle**: abstract runtime/agent boundaries, correlation hooks, and per-runtime caps; Hermes remains NOT ATTACHED/unauthorized | 4B.4–4B.6, runtime/adapter boundaries | (a) Design Hermes-specific scheduler now; (b) ignore future integration | Preserves boundaries and avoids speculative coupling | May need later adapter-specific refinement | Reduces rework/safety regressions | Avoids unused integration overhead | Maintains strict capability/flag gates | **Needed as constraint now**, integration **deferred** | High | Separately authorized Hermes capability expansion with new evidence |
